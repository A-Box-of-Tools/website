Apa yang akan datang

Semua yang ada di kedua daftar di bawah ini mengerjakan tugasnya di dalam peramban Anda dan tidak mengunggah apa pun — itulah aturan yang harus dilewati sebuah nama sebelum masuk ke sini sama sekali. Jumlahnya tidak dicatat di mana pun; proses build membacanya dari kedua daftar.

18 direncanakan · 31 selesai

Bagaimana daftar ini disusun

Ini bukan daftar keinginan. Daftar ini disusun dengan menelusuri daftar alat dari situs GIF dan video daring terbesar, hal yang paling mendekati katalog tentang apa yang sebenarnya ingin dilakukan orang terhadap sebuah file, dan menyimpan setiap entri yang bisa dibangun sehingga pekerjaannya terjadi di perangkat Anda sendiri dan terus berjalan meski koneksi jaringan diputus.

Sebagian besar yang tersisa berjalan dengan API yang sudah dimiliki peramban. Beberapa memerlukan codec yang disertakan langsung di satu alat yang menggunakannya, dan itu diperbolehkan — filenya tetap tidak pernah meninggalkan perangkat. Apa pun yang tidak lolos syarat ini ditinggalkan alih-alih dijanjikan, jadi beberapa kekosongan yang tampak jelas di bawah ini memang disengaja.

Alat yang sudah ada berada di bagian atas kelompoknya, ditandai Selesai dan tertaut ke halamannya sendiri. Semua yang ada di bawahnya masih akan datang, dan tidak akan menjadi tautan sampai benar-benar selesai.

Images

  • Image Compressor — Name the size. It works out the rest. Selesai
  • Image Resizer — Say the size. Draw the box. Pick the format. Selesai
  • HEIC to JPG — The photos an iPhone makes, in a format everything opens. Selesai
  • ID Photo Maker — Pick the country. It applies that country's rule, exactly. Selesai
  • Image Redactor — What you cover is deleted from the file, not covered up in it. Selesai
  • EXIF Viewer & Remover — See what a photo says about you. Then take it out. Selesai
  • DICOM Viewer — CT, MR, X-ray and ultrasound, with the window, the header and the measurements. Selesai
  • Image to ICO — One picture in. Every size a browser, Windows or a Mac asks for, out. Selesai
  • Image to Data URI — The whole picture as one line of text. Paste it straight into CSS or HTML. Selesai
  • SVG to Image — Name the size. A vector has none of its own to lose. Selesai
  • Add text — over the picture, or in a caption box above it
  • AVIF — the format one browser writes and the rest only read
  • DICOM anonymizer — take the patient out of a scan without touching the scan

GIF & animation

  • Video to GIF — Pick the section, the size, and the frame rate. Selesai
  • GIF Maker — Turn a set of pictures into one animation. Selesai
  • GIF Splitter — Every frame out as its own PNG. Selesai
  • GIF Analyzer — Frames, delays, palettes, and where every byte went. Selesai
  • GIF to MP4 or WebM — the same animation, usually a tenth of the size
  • Edit a GIF — resize, crop, rotate, reverse it, or change how fast it plays
  • Animated WebP — the same animation at a fraction of the size — and the one here that needs a vendored encoder

Video

  • Images to Video — Turn a folder of images into a video. Selesai
  • Video Cutter — Mark the parts worth keeping as it plays. Get them back as one video. Selesai
  • Video Cropper — Cut a clip down to the part that matters. Selesai
  • Video Reverser — Last frame first, sound and all. Selesai
  • Time-Lapse Maker — An hour of footage, in twenty seconds. Selesai
  • Video Frame Grabber — A full-quality still from any point. Selesai
  • Resize — down to a size that will actually send
  • Rotate — for the clip that was filmed sideways — and not one frame is decoded to do it
  • Mute a video — drop the sound and leave the picture untouched
  • Convert to MP4 — whatever came off the camera, as the file that will actually upload
  • Burn in subtitles — an SRT file drawn into the picture
  • Subtitle converter — SRT, VTT, ASS, SBV and LRC in any direction, and timings that can be shifted

Audio

  • Audio Trimmer — Mark the parts worth keeping as it plays. Get them back as one file, cut where you said. Selesai
  • Audio Editor — Play it backwards, change the speed, lift a quiet recording — all of it here, on your machine. Selesai
  • Convert format — MP3, WAV and Opus, in any direction

Documents & PDF

  • PDF Merger & Splitter — Pages moved around without a round trip to a server. Selesai
  • PDF Compressor — Shrink a document without sending it anywhere. Selesai
  • Images to PDF — Put your pictures into one document. Selesai
  • Document Scanner — Photograph the page. Get back something that looks scanned. Selesai
  • Rotate & crop pages — fix a scan that came in sideways, or trim its margins
  • Extract images — pull the pictures back out of a document
  • Redact a PDF — take the words out, rather than drawing a black box over them

Beyond media

  • Text & Code — Lay it out, see what changed, encode it, or turn it into the other format. Nothing is pasted into anyone else's server. Selesai
  • QR & Barcode Generator — Type it, and it becomes a code. Nothing is sent to make one. Selesai
  • QR & Barcode Reader — Point it at a code, or drop a picture of one. It is read here, and nowhere else. Selesai
  • Hash & Checksum — Check a download against the number the publisher printed, without sending it to anyone. Selesai
  • Password & Passphrase Generator — Made here, by your own browser, and never sent anywhere. Nothing is stored and there is no history. Selesai
  • CSV & JSON — convert either way, and tidy up what the spreadsheet exported
  • Certificates & keys — read a certificate or a signing request without pasting it into someone's server
