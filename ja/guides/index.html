作業を進める

ツールごとに一本。何をする作業なのか、どの設定が効くのか、そして何を犠牲にするのか。

  • How to compress an image to an exact file size An upload form wants 500 KB and your photo is 4 MB. What a size limit really costs, which setting to move first, and why a PNG will not shrink the way a JPEG does.
  • How to resize an image without wrecking it What happens to a picture when you change its pixel dimensions: why shrinking is safe and enlarging is not, what to do when the box is the wrong shape, and when to crop instead.
  • How to take a passport photo that is not sent back What a passport photo is actually measured on - head height, eye line, background - which country wants which numbers, and how to hit the pixel and KB limits an online form enforces.
  • How to make a favicon that still reads at sixteen pixels Which sizes a favicon.ico actually needs, which extra files iPhones, Android and a Mac ask for, and why a logo that works on a poster disappears at sixteen pixels.
  • How to convert an SVG to a PNG at the right size A vector has no pixel size of its own, so the number is yours to choose. Where that number comes from for a screen, an app icon and a printer, and what changes when a drawing becomes pixels.
  • When to put a picture inside your CSS, and when not to What a data URI costs, why base64 adds a third and gzip does not give it back, why an SVG should never be base64, and the quoting mistake that breaks inlined SVGs silently.
  • What a photo says about you, and how to take it out A photo off a phone usually carries the exact spot it was taken, the time to the second, and the camera's serial number. What is in there, who can read it, and how to take it out without touching the picture.
  • The photo your phone saved, and the format nothing will open iPhones save photos as HEIC, and half the internet cannot open one. What the format is, why only Safari decodes it, what converting costs the picture, and how to do it without uploading the photos to anyone.
  • How to merge, split and reorder PDF pages Combine PDFs, cut one into several, and move pages around: what survives the reshuffle, what no tool can carry across, and why none of it needs uploading your documents anywhere.
  • How to make a PDF smaller, and why some will not shrink Where a PDF's size actually is, why a scan compresses 80% and a contract barely moves, what DPI means here, and what a compressor should never do to your document.
  • How to combine images into one PDF Turn photos or scans into a single PDF: page size, order and rotation, why a JPEG need not lose any quality on the way in, and what a PDF tells the person you send it to.
  • How to turn a folder of images into a video Make an MP4 slideshow from photos: what frame rate and duration actually control, how to handle pictures that are the wrong shape, and why the result has no soundtrack.
  • How to make an animated GIF from images Turn a set of pictures into one animated GIF: how fast a GIF can really play, what the palette setting changes, and the three things that actually make the file smaller.
  • How to trim a video without re-encoding it Cutting a clip need not lose a single byte of quality. Why a cut sometimes lands earlier than you marked it, what a keyframe has to do with it, and when to accept a re-encode.
  • How to crop a video to a different shape Cut a clip down to a square, a 9:16 portrait, or an exact pixel box. Which ratio each platform wants, why cropping has to re-encode when trimming does not, and what that costs.
  • How to reverse a video Play a clip backwards: what reversing does to the picture and the sound, why it cannot be done without re-encoding, why it is slower than trimming, and what to do first.
  • How to save a frame from a video as a picture Get a still out of a clip at its real resolution: why a screenshot of a paused player is not the same picture, which format to save it in, and how to land on the exact frame you meant.
  • How to trim audio without losing quality Where an audio cut actually lands, why it can be exact when a video cut cannot, why a join sometimes clicks, and what a five-millisecond fade is really doing.
  • How to make a QR code that still scans on somebody else's phone Which error-correction level to pick, why the white margin around a QR code is part of the code, how big to print it, and what a free generator's 'dynamic' code costs you later.
  • How to turn a video into a GIF Which section, which width and which frame rate to choose, why a GIF of a video is ten times the size of the video, and when to use one at all.
  • How to split a GIF into frames Get every frame of an animated GIF as a PNG: why some frames are only a small patch of the picture, what happens to the transparency, and how to keep the timing so you can put it back together.
  • How to format JSON without handing it to anybody How to lay out, check and minify JSON in your own browser - what a formatter must never change about your file, how to read the error message, and why the site you paste it into matters.
