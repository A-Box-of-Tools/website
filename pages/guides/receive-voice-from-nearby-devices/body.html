Open the listening pager

Open Voice Oncall on the device that will listen. Choose Host a pager, enter a recognisable pager name and optionally set a password. Start pager prepares speaker playback and advertises the pager; it does not request microphone permission.

Use the website's HTTPS address. There is no installation, Python helper or account. Keep the host page open and its device awake while people are connected.

If the phone interrupts the host's sound, return to the page and choose Restore sound when shown. The pager stays open while sound is paused.

Let nearby people connect

Participants open the same tool and look for the pager in the nearby list. They enter a display name and the pager password when required, then choose Connect and allow microphone access. Admission is automatic: the host does not accept each person separately.

The list groups pages by their public internet address and this website's origin. That often matches people using the same connection, but it does not scan Wi-Fi or prove physical proximity. VPNs and separate routes can hide a nearby pager, while a shared carrier gateway can include strangers. Use the host's copied invitation or QR code when the list does not find it. Share passwords separately from the link.

Speak only when you intend to

A joined microphone starts with transmission stopped. Hold Talk, or focus the button and hold Space, to send live voice. Release it to stop. Start talking provides a hands-free toggle; stop it when you have finished.

Only the host receives participants' voice. Participants do not hear one another, and several simultaneous speakers can overlap at the host. Hiding a speaking page or a microphone interruption releases its microphone; use Resume microphone and deliberately start talking again. Disconnect closes the participant session and stops retries.

Choose who the host hears

The host's participant list shows names and speaking state. Mute silences one participant in the host browser, and Unmute allows that participant to be heard again. The normal speaking page also stops transmitting when muted; unmuting does not deliberately start its microphone.

Remove silences the participant locally while the service confirms removal, then closes its connection and blocks its retained session identity. If the service refuses the remembered action, the row stays visible and the host can choose Unmute again. Names are self-chosen and sessions are not accounts, so removal cannot permanently ban a person who creates a fresh identity. A password helps control admission. End pager closes the current pager; a new pager uses a fresh invitation and resets moderation history.

Understand the connection

Internet access is required for discovery, admission and connection setup. That service carries pager and participant names, password-derived credentials, moderation and network details, never voice. Audio travels on separate encrypted direct browser connections without a STUN server or public audio relay fallback.

Waiting participants check for the host with a one-second foreground target. Sleeping devices, background timers and connection limits can delay recovery. Guest isolation, firewalls, VPN routes or browser local-network permissions may prevent direct audio even when both devices are on the same Wi-Fi. Try the actual phones and computers you intend to use.

If your goal is for one person to speak to many listeners, use Local Walkie-Talkie instead. Voice Oncall receives many separate voices at one host.
