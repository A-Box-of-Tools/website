# Photo requirements: Any biometric travel document, ICAO standard

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from International Civil Aviation Organization, as it was read on 2026-08-20 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-08-20

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 70.0% to 80.0% (⁦31.5–36⁩ mm) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 60.0% (⁦22.5–27⁩ mm) |
| Background | Plain light grey |
| Machine-readable capture | at least ⁦413 x 531⁩ px, JPEG, no file-size rule stated |

## What else this rule says

- The ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm frame is what most of the world issues against, and 70-80 per cent head height with the eye line between half and three-fifths of the way up is the geometry every specification below is a variation of.
- Doc 9303 also asks for at least 90 pixels between the centres of the eyes in the stored image, which a ⁦413 x 531⁩ crop of a modern phone photograph clears comfortably.

## Where these figures come from

Transcribed from International Civil Aviation Organization — Doc 9303, Machine Readable Travel Documents, Part 3. Checked 2026-08-20. Rules change; the figures above are what to check against the form in front of you.
