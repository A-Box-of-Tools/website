# Photo requirements: Visa application, Schengen area

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from European Commission, as it was read on 2026-08-20 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-08-20

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 71.1% to 80.0% (⁦32–36⁩ mm) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 60.0% (⁦22.5–27⁩ mm) |
| Background | Plain light grey |
| Upload rule | none published — this one is a print |

## What else this rule says

- The common visa format used by every Schengen consulate: ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm with the face filling 70 to 80 per cent of the height, which is 32 to 36 mm.
- Consulates differ on what they will accept as a background. Plain light grey is refused by nobody; a white wall with a shadow on it is refused by most.

## Where these figures come from

Transcribed from European Commission — Visa Code, common photograph standards (ICAO-aligned). Checked 2026-08-20. Rules change; the figures above are what to check against the form in front of you.
