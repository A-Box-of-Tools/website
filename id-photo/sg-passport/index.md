# Photo requirements: Passport and identity card, Singapore

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Immigration & Checkpoints Authority, as it was read on 2026-09-17 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-17

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 70.0% to 80.0% (⁦31.5–36⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 60.0% (⁦22.5–27⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Background | Plain white |
| Online application photo | exactly ⁦400 x 514⁩ px, JPEG, up to 8.00 MB |

## What else this rule says

- What Singapore publishes is the upload: ⁦400 x 514⁩ pixels, up to 8 MB, on a white background and taken in the last three months rather than the usual six. No millimetres are stated anywhere — ⁦400 x 514⁩ is the ⁦35 x 45⁩ proportion exactly, which is where this print size comes from.
- No head-height band is published for this one, so the ICAO figures are shown in its place and are never marked as a failure. Frame it the way the rule describes in words and the numbers follow.

## Where these figures come from

Transcribed from Immigration & Checkpoints Authority — ica.gov.sg, Photo Guidelines. Checked 2026-09-17. Rules change; the figures above are what to check against the form in front of you.
