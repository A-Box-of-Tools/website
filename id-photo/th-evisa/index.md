# Photo requirements: e-Visa, Thailand

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Kingdom of Thailand, as it was read on 2026-09-19 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-19

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | no print size — this rule is for a web form |
| Head, chin to crown | 65.0% to 75.0% |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 62.0% (guidance) |
| Background | White or off-white |
| Online application photo | at least ⁦413 x 531⁩ px, JPEG, up to 3.00 MB |

## What else this rule says

- Thailand’s e-Visa publishes a head of around 70 per cent of the height, a vertical photograph on plain white or off-white, and a JPEG of at most 3 MB — and no size in pixels or millimetres. The shape written here is the usual ⁦35 x 45⁩ at 300 dpi, because some shape has to be; the portal offers an example photograph to compare against.
- No eye line is published, so the band shown is where the eyes fall on a head sitting centred in the frame. It is guidance rather than a requirement, and is never painted red.

## Where these figures come from

Transcribed from Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Kingdom of Thailand — Photograph Specifications (e-Visa), Guide to Thailand E-Visa Application. Checked 2026-09-19. Rules change; the figures above are what to check against the form in front of you.
