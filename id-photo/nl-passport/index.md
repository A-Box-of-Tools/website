# Photo requirements: Passport, identity card and driving licence, Netherlands

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Rijksdienst voor Identiteitsgegevens, as it was read on 2026-09-17 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm at 400 dpi |

| Background | White, light grey or light blue |

## What else this rule says

- The head band is 26 to 30 mm, which is shorter than it sounds: 58 to 67 per cent of the frame, where ICAO asks for 70 to 80. A crop made to the ICAO rule is too tight for a Dutch application, and this is the entry that catches it.
- Two more published figures are read and not used: the face must be 16 to 20 mm wide with the ears left out, and a child under 11 may go down to 19 mm of head height. The 400 dpi floor is the reason this one prints larger than the rest.
- No eye line is published, so the band shown is where the eyes fall on a head sitting centred in the frame. It is guidance rather than a requirement, and is never painted red.

## Where these figures come from

Transcribed from Rijksdienst voor Identiteitsgegevens — Fotomatrix 2020, photo specification guidelines. Checked 2026-09-17. Rules change; the figures above are what to check against the form in front of you.
