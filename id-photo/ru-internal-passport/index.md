# Photo requirements: Internal passport, Russia

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Министерство внутренних дел Российской Федерации, as it was read on 2026-09-21 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-21

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 60.0% to 80.0% (⁦27–36⁩ mm) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 60.0% (⁦22.5–27⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Background | Plain white |
| Upload rule | none published — this one is a print |

## What else this rule says

- Two prints of ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm on white, colour or black and white. The regulation caps the head at 80 per cent of the height and leaves 5 mm, give or take one, clear above it; the lower end of the band here is this tool’s. Somebody who always wears glasses must be photographed in them, without tinted lenses; no uniform, no coat, no scarf over the chin, and nothing edited to improve how anyone looks. Read from the published text of the interior ministry’s order, whose own site does not answer from here.
- No eye line is published, so the band shown is where the eyes fall on a head sitting centred in the frame. It is guidance rather than a requirement, and is never painted red.

## Where these figures come from

Transcribed from Министерство внутренних дел Российской Федерации — Приказ МВД России от 16.11.2020 N 773, пункт 36. Checked 2026-09-21. Rules change; the figures above are what to check against the form in front of you.

## What the tool does with them

- Locks the crop to this document’s shape, and draws the head and eye bands inside it, so you can see the rule on your own photograph instead of reading about it.
- Finds the crown, the chin and both pupils by measuring the picture — no face model is downloaded — and says in millimetres whether the head is too large or too small for this rule.
- Reads the background colour and how evenly it is lit, and says which of the two is the problem when there is one.
- Writes the print at the exact millimetres with the resolution in the file, a sheet of them with cut marks, and the upload file at the pixel and file size this rule states.
