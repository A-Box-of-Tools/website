# Photo requirements: Passport and identity card, Poland

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Ministerstwo Spraw Wewnętrznych i Administracji, as it was read on 2026-09-17 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-17

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 68.9% to 80.0% (⁦31–36⁩ mm) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 44.4% to 66.7% (⁦20–30⁩ mm) |
| Background | Plain white or light grey |
| Online application photo | at least ⁦492 x 633⁩ px, JPEG, up to 2.50 MB |

## What else this rule says

- This rule measures the head to the top of the skull, with the hair left out of it. The dots here find the top of the hair, because that is what a photograph has in it, so on a full head of hair the measurement reads high — aim at the lower half of the band.
- Poland publishes the eye line in millimetres up from the bottom edge, which almost nobody does: 20 to 30 mm. It also says that on a full head of hair some of the hairstyle may fall outside the frame, so long as the face itself is not made smaller.
- An identity card can be applied for online with a file of at least ⁦492 x 633⁩ pixels and 2.5 MB at most, which is the upload written here. A passport cannot, except for a child under twelve, and that one asks for ⁦768 x 1004⁩.
