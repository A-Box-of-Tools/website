# Photo requirements: Driving licence, Indonesia

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Korps Lalu Lintas Polri, as it was read on 2026-09-21 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-21

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | no print size — this rule is for a web form |
| Head, chin to crown | 55.0% to 75.0% (guidance) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 65.0% (guidance) |
| Background | Plain blue |
| Online application photo | exactly ⁦480 x 640⁩ px, JPEG, up to 100.0 KB |

## What else this rule says

- Renewing a SIM online takes exactly ⁦480 x 640⁩ pixels and 100 KB at most, on blue. No glasses or contact lenses, no head covering, no blue hijab — it would vanish into the background — and the original file, not a picture of a print. A first licence is photographed at the office.
- No head-height band is published for this one, and its frame is not the usual one, so ICAO’s figures do not carry over. The band shown is a loose one read out of what the rule says in words; it is guidance and is never marked as a failure.

## Where these figures come from

Transcribed from Korps Lalu Lintas Polri — Digital Korlantas, perpanjangan SIM. Checked 2026-09-21. Rules change; the figures above are what to check against the form in front of you.
