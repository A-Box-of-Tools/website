# Photo requirements: Driving licence, Vietnam

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Cục Cảnh sát giao thông, Bộ Công an, as it was read on 2026-09-21 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-21

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦30 x 40⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 65.0% to 80.0% (⁦26–32⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 62.0% (⁦20–24.8⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Background | Plain white |
| Online application photo | at least ⁦473 x 630⁩ px, JPEG, up to 700.0 KB |

## What else this rule says

- For exchanging or replacing a licence online: a ⁦3 x 4⁩ cm photograph on white — the police portal says white, whatever a provincial page says about blue — of at least ⁦473 x 630⁩ pixels and 700 KB at most. Avoid white and black clothes, keep the head within five degrees of upright, and take thick-rimmed or dark glasses off.
- No head-height band is published for this one, and its frame is not the usual one, so ICAO’s figures do not carry over. The band shown is a loose one read out of what the rule says in words; it is guidance and is never marked as a failure.
