# Photo requirements: Passport, Pakistan

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Directorate General of Immigration & Passports, as it was read on 2026-09-19 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-19

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 64.4% to 75.6% (⁦29–34⁩ mm) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 60.0% (⁦22.5–27⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Background | Plain white |
| Online application photo | at least ⁦413 x 531⁩ px, JPEG, up to 5.00 MB |

## What else this rule says

- Read this one before using anything made here. Pakistan asks for a professionally taken photograph and says in so many words that one “cut down from larger pictures” or edited in software will not do — which is what a crop is. Use this to check that a studio’s photograph meets the 29 to 34 mm head and the white background; the upload may be up to 5 MB and no pixel size is stated, so the one written is the print at 300 dpi.
- No eye line is published, so the band shown is where the eyes fall on a head sitting centred in the frame. It is guidance rather than a requirement, and is never painted red.

## Where these figures come from

Transcribed from Directorate General of Immigration & Passports — e-Services Portal, Photograph Requirements. Checked 2026-09-19. Rules change; the figures above are what to check against the form in front of you.
