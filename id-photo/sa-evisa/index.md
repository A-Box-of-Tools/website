# Photo requirements: e-Visa, Saudi Arabia

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Saudi eVisa, visa.visitsaudi.com, as it was read on 2026-09-19 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

## What else this rule says

- The Saudi tourist e-Visa wants a square of exactly ⁦200 x 200⁩ pixels, between 5 and 100 KB, on white, with the face filling 70 to 80 per cent of it and both ears showing. The same sentence also gives the head in inches, which a file of 200 pixels has none of; the percentage is what is applied here. At that size nothing survives but the face, so start from a sharp photograph.
- No eye line is published, so the band shown is where the eyes fall on a head sitting centred in the frame. It is guidance rather than a requirement, and is never painted red.
