# Photo requirements: Passport and identity card, France

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Service-Public.fr / Ministère chargé de l’intérieur, as it was read on 2026-09-17 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-17

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 71.1% to 80.0% (⁦32–36⁩ mm) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 60.0% (⁦22.5–27⁩ mm) |
| Background | Light grey or light blue — not white |
| Upload rule | none published — this one is a print |

## What else this rule says

- This rule measures the head to the top of the skull, with the hair left out of it. The dots here find the top of the hair, because that is what a photograph has in it, so on a full head of hair the measurement reads high — aim at the lower half of the band.
- The photograph has to be taken by an approved photographer or in an approved booth, so a print made here is for checking that theirs is right rather than for handing in.

## Where these figures come from

Transcribed from Service-Public.fr / Ministère chargé de l’intérieur — Quelle photo fournir pour un titre d’identité ?. Checked 2026-09-17. Rules change; the figures above are what to check against the form in front of you.
