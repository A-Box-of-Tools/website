# Photo requirements: Firearms licence (PAL), Canada

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Royal Canadian Mounted Police, as it was read on 2026-09-21 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-21

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦45 x 57⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 52.6% to 78.9% (⁦30–45⁩ mm) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 68.0% (⁦28.5–38.8⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Background | White or a plain light colour |
| Online application photo | at least ⁦531 x 673⁩ px, JPEG, 200.0 KB to 6.00 MB |

## What else this rule says

- A size of its own, ⁦45 x 57⁩ mm, with a head of at least 30 mm and no maximum published — the upper end of the band here is this tool’s. The file must be a JPG between 200 KB and 6 MB. It may be a year old and in black and white, tinted prescription lenses are refused even when the eyes show, and on a first application a guarantor signs the back of the print.
- No eye line is published, so the band shown is where the eyes fall on a head sitting centred in the frame. It is guidance rather than a requirement, and is never painted red.

## Where these figures come from

Transcribed from Royal Canadian Mounted Police — Photo requirements for a firearms licence. Checked 2026-09-21. Rules change; the figures above are what to check against the form in front of you.

## What the tool does with them

- Locks the crop to this document’s shape, and draws the head and eye bands inside it, so you can see the rule on your own photograph instead of reading about it.
- Finds the crown, the chin and both pupils by measuring the picture — no face model is downloaded — and says in millimetres whether the head is too large or too small for this rule.
- Reads the background colour and how evenly it is lit, and says which of the two is the problem when there is one.
- Writes the print at the exact millimetres with the resolution in the file, a sheet of them with cut marks, and the upload file at the pixel and file size this rule states.
