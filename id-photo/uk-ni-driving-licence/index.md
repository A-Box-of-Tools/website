## What else this rule says

- Northern Ireland publishes its own rule; Great Britain’s DVLA asks only for “a recent printed passport type photo”, which is the passport entry. Here the photograph may be no more than a month old, on light grey or cream, in colour, and the page says not to trim a print down to ⁦45 x 35⁩ mm and that one printed at home is unlikely to be good enough. Applying online is different again: “don’t crop your photo — it will be done for you”, so send that one as it came off the camera.
- No head-height band is published for this one, and its frame is not the usual one, so ICAO’s figures do not carry over. The band shown is a loose one read out of what the rule says in words; it is guidance and is never marked as a failure.
