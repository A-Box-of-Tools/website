## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 71.1% to 80.0% (⁦32–36⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 60.0% (⁦22.5–27⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Background | Plain and light — no colour is published |
| Online application photo | at least ⁦480 x 617⁩ px, JPEG, 20.0 KB to 7.00 MB |

## What else this rule says

- By post it is a ⁦45 x 35⁩ mm print with your name and date of birth written on the back; online it is a JPEG in RGB between 20 KB and 7 MB, each side from 480 to 6000 pixels, and no shape is stated — the one written here is the print’s. The diagram’s 34 mm face, give or take two, is labelled a guide, so it is shown as guidance. Black and white is accepted. A picture run through editing software may be refused, so check it against the examples on the site.
- No head-height band is published for this one, and its frame is not the usual one, so ICAO’s figures do not carry over. The band shown is a loose one read out of what the rule says in words; it is guidance and is never marked as a failure.
