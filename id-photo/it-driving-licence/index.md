# Photo requirements: Driving licence, Italy

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Ministero delle Infrastrutture e dei Trasporti, Motorizzazione, as it was read on 2026-09-21 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-21

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 62.2% to 71.1% (⁦28–32⁩ mm) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 62.0% (⁦22.5–27.9⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Background | White or a plain light colour |

## What else this rule says

- The ministry’s circular states the size as a range, 40 to 45 mm high by 32 to 35 wide, and the head twice over: 60 to 90 per cent of the height in its text and 28 to 32 mm under its figure. The millimetres sit inside the percentages and are what is applied here. Background light grey, cream or light blue for preference, or white; matt paper is advised; several photographs for one application must be identical.
- No eye line is published, so the band shown is where the eyes fall on a head sitting centred in the frame. It is guidance rather than a requirement, and is never painted red.
