# Photo requirements: Driving licence, Denmark

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Færdselsstyrelsen, as it was read on 2026-09-21 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-21

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 66.7% to 80.0% (⁦30–36⁩ mm) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 62.0% (⁦22.5–27.9⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Background | White or a plain light colour |
| Upload rule | none published — this one is a print |

## What else this rule says

- Denmark measures the head to the top of the hair, 30 to 36 mm, which is how this tool measures it, so there is no correction to make. A plain light background — light blue or light grey are its examples — and one in sharp contrast for white or fair hair or a light headscarf. Colour or black and white.
- No eye line is published, so the band shown is where the eyes fall on a head sitting centred in the frame. It is guidance rather than a requirement, and is never painted red.

## Where these figures come from

- Writes the print at the exact millimetres with the resolution in the file, a sheet of them with cut marks, and the upload file at the pixel and file size this rule states.
