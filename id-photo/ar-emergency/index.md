# Photo requirements: Emergency or provisional passport, Argentina

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Consulado General de la República Argentina en Roma, as it was read on 2026-09-19 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-19

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦40 x 40⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 50.0% to 70.0% (⁦20–28⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 65.0% (⁦20–26⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Background | Plain white |
| Upload rule | none published — this one is a print |

## What else this rule says

- Square and small: ⁦4 x 4⁩ cm, facing front on white, and “medio busto” — the shoulders are in the picture, so the head is smaller than in a passport crop. No hat and no glasses at all. It is what a consulate asks for when a passport has been lost or stolen abroad; the consulate in Asunción adds that it be in colour and on matt paper.
- No head-height band is published for this one, and its frame is not the usual one, so ICAO’s figures do not carry over. The band shown is a loose one read out of what the rule says in words; it is guidance and is never marked as a failure.

## Where these figures come from

Transcribed from Consulado General de la República Argentina en Roma — Requisitos para la tramitación de pasaporte argentino. Checked 2026-09-19. Rules change; the figures above are what to check against the form in front of you.
