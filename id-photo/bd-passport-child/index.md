## What else this rule says

- A child under six is not enrolled at the office, so a photograph is sent instead — and not a passport-size one. The rule asks for a 3R lab print, three and a half inches by five, on a grey background; the office takes the face out of it itself, which is why the head band here is wide.
- No head-height band is published for this one, and its frame is not the usual one, so ICAO’s figures do not carry over. The band shown is a loose one read out of what the rule says in words; it is guidance and is never marked as a failure.
