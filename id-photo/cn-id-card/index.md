# Photo requirements: Identity card, China

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from 北京市公安局, as it was read on 2026-09-21 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-21

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦26 x 32⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 60.0% to 72.0% (⁦19.2–23⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 62.0% (⁦16–19.8⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Background | Plain white |
| Upload rule | none published — this one is a print |

## What else this rule says

- The identity card is smaller than any passport size: ⁦26 x 32⁩ mm, on white with no border, the head about two thirds of the picture and the face 15 mm wide, give or take one, between the roots of the ears. No uniform and no white top; somebody who always wears glasses wears them. Most police stations take this photograph themselves or send you to a studio they appoint — ask before printing one.
- No head-height band is published for this one, and its frame is not the usual one, so ICAO’s figures do not carry over. The band shown is a loose one read out of what the rule says in words; it is guidance and is never marked as a failure.

## Where these figures come from

Transcribed from 北京市公安局 — 居民身份证照片标准，引《居民身份证制证用数字相片技术要求》GA461—2004. Checked 2026-09-21. Rules change; the figures above are what to check against the form in front of you.
