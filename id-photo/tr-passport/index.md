# Photo requirements: Passport, identity card and driving licence, Türkiye

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Nüfus ve Vatandaşlık İşleri Genel Müdürlüğü, as it was read on 2026-09-19 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-19

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦50 x 60⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 70.0% to 80.0% (⁦42–48⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 60.0% (⁦30–36⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Background | Plain white |
| Upload rule | none published — this one is a print |

## What else this rule says

- Not a ⁦35 x 45⁩, and the authority says so in as many words: the biometric photograph is ⁦50 x 60⁩ mm and photographers are to be reminded not to cut it down. A different size or background stops the application, or gets the card cancelled at quality control afterwards.
- No head-height band is published for this one, so the ICAO figures are shown in its place and are never marked as a failure. Frame it the way the rule describes in words and the numbers follow.
