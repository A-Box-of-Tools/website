# Photo requirements: Expatriate pass (ESD Online), Malaysia

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from Jabatan Imigresen Malaysia, Expatriate Services Division, as it was read on 2026-09-19 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

Last updated 2026-09-19

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦35 x 50⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 60.0% to 70.0% (⁦30–35⁩ mm) |
| Eye line, from the bottom | 50.0% to 62.0% (⁦25–31⁩ mm) (guidance) |
| Background | Plain white |
| Online application photo | at least ⁦413 x 591⁩ px, JPEG, up to 25.0 KB |

[Make this photo](https://abox.tools/id-photo/#my-expat-pass) Opens the photo tool with this rule already chosen. It runs in your browser: the photograph is never uploaded, and there is no account and no watermark.

## What else this rule says

- The same ⁦35 x 50⁩ mm frame as the Malaysian passport and a bigger head in it — 30 to 35 mm against the passport’s 25 to 30 — so one crop does not serve both. The upload may be no larger than 25 KB, which is little enough that the file written here is squeezed until it fits. White is accepted for both kinds of application the page names and blue for only one of them, so white is what is checked.
- No eye line is published, so the band shown is where the eyes fall on a head sitting centred in the frame. It is guidance rather than a requirement, and is never painted red.

## Where these figures come from

Transcribed from Jabatan Imigresen Malaysia, Expatriate Services Division — ESD Online, Passport Photo Requirements. Checked 2026-09-19. Rules change; the figures above are what to check against the form in front of you.

## What the tool does with them

- Locks the crop to this document’s shape, and draws the head and eye bands inside it, so you can see the rule on your own photograph instead of reading about it.
- Finds the crown, the chin and both pupils by measuring the picture — no face model is downloaded — and says in millimetres whether the head is too large or too small for this rule.
- Reads the background colour and how evenly it is lit, and says which of the two is the problem when there is one.
- Writes the print at the exact millimetres with the resolution in the file, a sheet of them with cut marks, and the upload file at the pixel and file size this rule states.
