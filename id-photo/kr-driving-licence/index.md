# Photo requirements: Driving licence, South Korea

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from 한국도로교통공단, as it was read on 2026-09-21 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

## The figures

| What is measured | What this rule publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Print size | ⁦35 x 45⁩ mm at 300 dpi |
| Head, chin to crown | 71.1% to 80.0% (⁦32–36⁩ mm) |

## What else this rule says

- The print is the passport’s, 32 to 36 mm from the top of the head to the chin, on white, on photo paper only, with no teeth showing and nothing edited. Online the rule is unusually exact: a JPG of 500 KB at most, ⁦413 x 531⁩ pixels asked for and nothing outside 395 to 431 by 507 to 550 accepted at all.
- No eye line is published, so the band shown is where the eyes fall on a head sitting centred in the frame. It is guidance rather than a requirement, and is never painted red.
