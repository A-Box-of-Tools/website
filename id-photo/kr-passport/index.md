# Photo requirements: Passport, South Korea

The published rule for this document’s photograph — from 외교부 (Ministry of Foreign Affairs), as it was read on 2026-09-17 — and a way to make one that meets it without sending your face to anybody.

## What else this rule says

- This rule measures the head to the top of the skull, with the hair left out of it. The dots here find the top of the hair, because that is what a photograph has in it, so on a full head of hair the measurement reads high — aim at the lower half of the band.
- Korea checks two things almost nobody else states outright: the background may not be cut out or replaced in software, and the head is measured to the top of the skull with the hair excluded. The online form recommends ⁦413 x 531⁩ pixels, which is this print at 300 dpi.
- No eye line is published, so the band shown is where the eyes fall on a head sitting centred in the frame. It is guidance rather than a requirement, and is never painted red.
