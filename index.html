🧰 abox.tools

A box of tools that never touch a server

Small, single-purpose utilities that do all of their work inside your browser. Your files stay on your machine: there is nothing to upload, no account to make, and nothing to track.

  • Your files never leave your machine They are opened, read, and processed by your own browser, using your own hardware. Each tool's Content-Security-Policy names every address it may contact, and not one of them belongs to us.
  • Nothing about your files is sent anywhere Not the file, not a thumbnail, not the name, the size, or how many you chose. No tool here reads any of that out. There is no server on the other end of these tools to read it.
  • It works offline Open a tool once, disconnect from the internet, and it keeps working on your files exactly as before. That is hard to fake: a tool that sent your files somewhere to be processed would simply stop.

Images

  • Resize one image or a whole folder, by pixels or by percent
  • Crop free, fixed ratio, or an exact pixel box
  • Rotate & flip quarter turns, or straightening by a few degrees
  • Convert format PNG, JPG and WebP, in any direction
  • Compress a quality dial, with the new size shown before you save
  • Add text over the picture, or in a caption box above it
  • Filters brightness, contrast, saturation, blur, grayscale
  • Image to data URI paste the result straight into CSS or HTML
  • Metadata viewer read what a photo says about you, without sending it anywhere
  • Metadata remover EXIF, GPS and camera details out of a photo
  • PNG to ICO a favicon at every size a browser asks for
  • SVG to PNG rasterize a vector at any size
  • HEIC to JPG the photos an iPhone makes, in a format everything opens
  • AVIF & JPEG XL convert either way, in whichever browser you happen to use

GIF & animation

  • GIF maker images into an animated GIF
  • Video to GIF pick the section, the size, and the frame rate
  • GIF to MP4 or WebM the same animation, usually a tenth of the size
  • Split a GIF every frame out as its own PNG
  • Resize, crop & rotate GIFs frame by frame, with the timing kept
  • Reverse a GIF last frame first
  • Change GIF speed retime it without re-rendering the frames
  • GIF analyzer frames, delays, palette, and where the bytes went
  • APNG maker animation with real transparency and full color
  • Animated WebP the same animation again, at a fraction of the size

Video

  • Trim cut a section out and keep the original quality
  • Resize down to a size that will actually send
  • Crop including squaring up a clip for a feed
  • Rotate for the clip that was filmed sideways
  • Reverse play it backwards
  • Mute, or save the audio drop the sound, or keep it on its own
  • Grab a frame a full-quality still from any point
  • Convert to MP4 or WebM whichever one the site you are posting to insists on
  • Filters brightness, contrast, saturation, blur
  • Burn in subtitles an SRT file drawn into the picture

Audio

  • Trim keep the part you wanted
  • Fade in & out so it does not start and stop on a cliff
  • Change speed with or without moving the pitch
  • Normalize or boost volume bring a quiet recording up to a usable level
  • Waveform image draw a track as a picture
  • Convert format MP3, WAV and Opus, in any direction

  • Merge, split & reorder pages moved around without a round trip to a server
  • Rotate & crop pages fix a scan that came in sideways, or trim its margins
  • Extract images pull the pictures back out of a document

Beyond media

  • QR & barcodes generated from a string, with nothing sent to make one
