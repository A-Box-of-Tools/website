🧰 abox.tools

A box of tools that never touch a server

Small, single-purpose utilities that do all of their work inside your browser. Your files stay on your machine: there is nothing to upload, no account to make, and nothing to track.

  • Nothing is uploaded Each tool sets connect-src 'none', which switches off fetch, XHR, WebSocket and sendBeacon. The browser enforces it, not the code.
  • Nothing is loaded from elsewhere No CDN, no web fonts, no analytics. Every file a page uses is served from this origin, so there is no third party to trust.
  • It works offline Open a tool once, disconnect from the internet, and it keeps working. That is hard to fake — a tool that needed a server would stop.

Images & video

Work with pictures and footage without handing them to anyone.

  • 🎞️ Images to Video Turn a folder of images into an MP4 slideshow. Encoding runs on your own machine, using your own hardware.
