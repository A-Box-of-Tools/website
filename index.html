🧰 abox.tools

A box of tools that never touch a server

Small, single-purpose tools that do all of their work inside your browser. Your files stay on your machine: there is nothing to upload, no account to make, and nothing to track.

These tools are free and always will be. If one saved you a job, this is the only thing on the site that asks you for anything.

  • Your files never leave your machine They are opened, read, and processed by your own browser, using your own hardware. Each tool's Content-Security-Policy names every address it may contact, and not one of them belongs to us.
  • Nothing about your files is sent anywhere Not the file, not a thumbnail, not the name, the size, or how many you chose. No tool here reads any of that out. There is no server on the other end of these tools to read it.
  • It works offline Open a tool once, disconnect from the internet, and it keeps working on your files exactly as before. That is hard to fake: a tool that sent your files somewhere to be processed would simply stop.

None of that has to be taken on trust. How to tell whether a tool really needs your files sets out four checks you can run yourself, on this site or any other.

Images & video

Work with pictures and footage without handing them to anyone.

  • 🎞️ Images to Video Turn a folder of images into an MP4 slideshow. Encoding runs on your own machine, using your own hardware.
  • 🎞️ Video Trimmer Mark a start and an end and cut — or take a piece out of the middle. The frames are copied rather than re-encoded, so nothing is lost.
  • ✂️ Video Cropper Drag a box over a clip and cut it down to that shape — square for a feed, tall for a phone, or an exact pixel box.
  • 🏷️ EXIF Viewer & Remover See the location, camera and serial numbers hidden in a photo, edit any of it, or strip the lot in one click.
  • 🗜️ Image Compressor Say the size you need — 100 KB, 2 MB, anything — and it finds the least compression that gets there.

Documents & PDF

Make and rearrange documents without sending them to be processed.

  • 📈 PDF Compressor Make a document smaller by recompressing the pictures inside it. It shows you where the size actually is first, and says plainly when there is nothing to be had.
  • 📄 Images to PDF Combine images into one PDF, one picture per page. JPEG photos are copied in exactly as they are, so nothing loses quality.
