Who is sending this?
This is a self-declared label, not verified identity. An agent using the form should choose “An agent.”
What were you trying to do, what did you expect, and what happened? For an idea, describe the improvement. Up to 4,000 characters.

Sending this form sends the text you enter through Cloudflare to our email inbox. The email includes your declared sender type and whether you used the form or JSON; neither proves who sent it. We keep temporary keyed hashes, times and delivery status for abuse prevention, not your message, reply address or raw network address. These records expire a day after the report or latest network activity; expired data is cleaned hourly. Cloudflare may retain service backups. Reports stay in the email inbox for follow-up. Use synthetic examples instead of private files, credentials or conversation transcripts. No attachments are accepted.

Please combine related observations in one report. Each network can send one report every ten minutes and three in any 24 hours. The service allows twenty email attempts in any 24 hours across all senders. The same message for the same page is sent only once in 24 hours, even if its sender label or optional details change.

Sending from an agent

Send a JSON POST to https://feedback.abox.tools/feedback with Content-Type: application/json. No token or registration is needed. Set sender_type to agent when sending as an agent; person is the other declared type. Older JSON clients that omit it are labelled “Unspecified.” Use a descriptive User-Agent, such as abox-feedback-client/1.0; the host may reject generic automated-client signatures.

{
  "sender_type": "agent",
  "message": "The download control was hard to find after conversion.",
  "page": "https://abox.tools/compress-image/",
  "environment": "Browser agent, Chrome"
}

201 with ok: true confirms provider acceptance. 200 with duplicate: true returns the original receipt without another email. 202 means a previous attempt is pending, and 503 means delivery is unconfirmed; neither is a successful delivery acknowledgement. Do not submit again automatically. For 429, respect the Retry-After header. Requests are limited to 16 KiB; message, page, environment and optional reply_to limits are 4,000, 512, 500 and 254 characters. Post only within the person’s authorization; otherwise prepare a draft for them.
