2 Say how big it may be

The sound is copied as it is and the container takes a little, so what is left over the length of the clip is what the picture may spend. The picture is then shrunk until every frame gets enough of it to look like one, down a ladder of the sizes people know, never up.

MB

Frame size

A rung is a ceiling: the picture is never made larger than it came, and a big frame at a small number is a smeared frame.
