How did we work this out?

The entered count is exact. Physical sizes and writing equivalents are estimates. The drawings are counting pictures; different object types are not drawn at the same physical scale.

Chosen spherical grains, with 60% grains and 40% air. Real sand has mixed shapes and sizes.

English text: roughly 0.75 words per token. This is a writing equivalent, not a claim about books actually read or written.

Counting continuously, without sleep or breaks. One year is 365.25 days.

Container capacities are chosen examples: a 15 mL spoon, a 250 mL cup, a 10 L bucket, a 200 L bath, a 5 × 3 × 2 m room, and a 50 × 25 × 2 m pool. The example mountain is a cone 1 km tall with a 1 km base radius; it is not a measurement of a real mountain.

There is no single exact count for “all the sand on Earth” here. A defensible estimate first needs a scope, such as beaches or deserts, and assumptions about grain size and depth.

Read the references: OpenAI’s English token estimate · USGS grain-size classification. These links are optional reading; the calculator does not contact them.
