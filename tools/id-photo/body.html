1 Choose the photograph

Any picture your browser can open. A phone photograph taken against a plain wall, in daylight, from about a metre and a half away is what most of these rules were written around — and it is read straight off your disk, not sent anywhere.

    3 Mark the face, and check the framing

    Choose a photograph and the crop box appears here.

    The crown, the chin and each pupil. Those four points are the whole of what a rule measures, so the crop is taken from wherever they end up — drag any that landed wrong, then press Fit the box. Finding them reads the picture itself: the edge of your head against the wall behind it, and the darker patches where eyes are. There is no model in it and nothing is fetched, and it is a starting position rather than a verdict, which is why it is yours to correct and why the panel below says which of the four it could not measure.
