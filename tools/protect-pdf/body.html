2 Choose the password and the restrictions

The password is the lock: the document is encrypted with a key made from it, and nobody without it reads a word. The restrictions underneath are different — a request that readers honour by agreement, and that any reader may decline. You can set either, or both.

There is no way back in without this password. Nothing here guesses one, and nothing honest anywhere does either. Keep your original.

Restrictions

These ask a reader not to do something. Most readers listen; none is made to, and a document that opens without a password can always have them lifted. Tick them to say what you would like, not to make sure.
