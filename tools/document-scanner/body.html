1 Choose the photos

A photo of each page, taken from anywhere above it. The whole page has to be in the frame, and the four corners want to be visible or nearly so — everything else, the angle, the shadow, the desk it is lying on, is what this tool is for. Add several and they become the pages of one document, in the order you add them.

    2 Put the corners on the page

    Choose a photo and it appears here, with its four corners found for you.

    Press anywhere on the photo and the nearest corner comes to your finger; drag it onto the corner of the page. Tab reaches a corner from the keyboard, the arrow keys move it one pixel and Shift ten. Nothing here is final — the scan is taken from wherever the four corners end up.

    3 Clean it up

    The straightened page appears here once there is a photo to straighten.

    What is below is the real result, produced by the same code that writes the file — straightened, and with the uneven light divided out. It is drawn at screen size while you choose; the file itself is made at the photo's own resolution.
