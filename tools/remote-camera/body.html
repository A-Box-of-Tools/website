    How cameras are listed

    Listed cameras share the internet address seen by our introduction service and this site’s address. A shared VPN or carrier network can include strangers, and nearby cameras can be missed. Choose a camera you recognise.

    Both devices need this page open, an internet connection for pairing, and a local network that lets them reach each other. Video travels directly between the browsers. No microphone, recording or internet relay.

    Connection details and troubleshooting

    No camera connection yet.

    This tool offers only the browsers’ own host addresses, without public STUN servers or TURN relays. A VPN or operating-system route can affect the path. Guest Wi-Fi isolation, a firewall, blocked local address discovery, or browser and OS local-network permissions can prevent a connection.

    If pairing fails, stop on both devices, check that both are on the same reachable network, allow local-network access if asked, and try a new link. The camera device needs trusted HTTPS; ordinary HTTP on a LAN IP cannot request a camera.
