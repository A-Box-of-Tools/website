1 Choose a photo of each side

One photo of the front of the card and one of the back, taken from above with the whole card in the frame. It does not have to be square-on or cropped: finding the card and straightening it is what this tool is for. Drop both at once and the first is taken as the front.

2 Put the corners on the card

Press anywhere on a photo and the nearest corner comes to your finger; drag it onto the corner of the card. Tab reaches a corner from the keyboard, the arrow keys move it one pixel and Shift ten. For a card with rounded corners, put each corner where its two edges would meet.
