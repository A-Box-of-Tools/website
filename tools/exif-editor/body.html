    2 Remove everything

    One button, every photo on the list. For JPEG, PNG and WebP, the picture is not decoded, re-drawn or re-compressed — the compressed image data is copied across untouched and only the metadata around it is dropped, so the result is the same photo with nothing attached.

    AVIF is decoded by your browser and saved as a new PNG without copying the original metadata. Its EXIF tags can be viewed here but cannot be edited. The PNG may be larger, and conversion can change colour or HDR rendering. Your original file stays unchanged.

      3 Look inside, and change what you like

      Choose a photo above and its readable metadata appears here: what it says about where you were, when, and on what. Nothing is sent anywhere to work that out.

      AVIF preview shows only the EXIF block this reader can extract. XMP and other AVIF metadata are not inventoried. Use Remove all metadata to convert the decoded picture to PNG without copying original metadata.
