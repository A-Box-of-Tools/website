Already have a listing? Start from it

This page cannot go and fetch your profile — it has no way to contact anybody, which is the point of it. So bring the profile here instead: open your listing on Google, select it, copy it, and paste it below. Whatever is recognised is filled in and reported back field by field, so a wrong guess is visible rather than quietly signed off.

Names, ratings, categories, addresses, phone numbers, websites and a full week of opening hours are recognised. Photographs are not: a picture cannot travel through a clipboard as text, so add yours in step 1.

A saved profile is the JSON this page writes from step 4. It also reads a location straight out of a Google Business Profile API export — the title, storefrontAddress, regularHours shape — which is what an export of your own listing looks like.

1 Describe the business

Every box here is a field Google shows somewhere, and the preview at the foot of the page redraws as you type. The two worth the most attention are the name and the category: the name is the only field that is cut short on all three surfaces, and the category is the line a search result has room for when it has room for nothing else.

One line, in Google's own words for it. This is the phrase most searches actually match against.

Leave the rating and the review count empty and the card shows what a new listing shows: a line saying there are no reviews yet, rather than five empty stars. That is deliberate — five empty stars is what one star looks like at a glance, and this page exists so that nothing about your own listing surprises you.

Separated by commas — Dine-in, Takeaway, Wheelchair accessible. Six is as many as a card shows.

2 Where it is, and how to reach it

These three decide which buttons the listing gets. No address, no Directions; no website, no Website; no phone number, no Call. It is the fastest thing to check on the preview and the one most often missing from a real profile.

3 When it is open

The one line on a listing that changes by itself. Fill the week in and the preview works out what it says at this moment on your own clock — Open · Closes 9 PM, or Closed · Opens 9 AM Tue — which is the only way to find out what somebody looking you up on a Sunday evening is told.
