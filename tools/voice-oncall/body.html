    How nearby voices connect

    An internet service lists rooms in your internet-address group and introduces their browsers. This is not a scan of your Wi-Fi, and a listed room is not guaranteed to be on your physical network.

    Voice travels directly between browsers and is encrypted. There is no audio relay or recording. Use a trusted network where devices can reach one another; guest Wi-Fi, firewalls, and some VPNs may prevent connections.

    Anyone who can find your room can try to join. A password limits access, and the host can mute or remove participants. Senders need microphone permission on a secure page. The host listens without microphone access.

    Remove ends one participant session. A new tab or cleared browser storage can create a new identity, so removal is not a permanent ban. This tab remembers a removed or ended session and will not automatically create a replacement identity.
