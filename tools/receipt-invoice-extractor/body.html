1 Add receipt or invoice pictures

One document per picture. Add up to 20 JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF images, 20 MB each. English text is read on this device, using an OCR engine included with the page. AVIF pictures use your browser’s decoder.

{% include "partials/file-picker.html" %}

Keep the whole document in view, upright and in good light. For HEIC pictures, use HEIC to JPG first. PDFs and handwritten receipts are not supported.

2 Check each document

Check the crop and make sure the attachment is readable. OCR can misread digits or pick the wrong total: compare each suggestion with the picture, correct the fields, choose the final email currency and review its exchange rate, then tick “I checked this document”. Each picture counts as one document; adding it twice counts it twice.

OCR, cropping and manual conversion stay on this device. Historical rate lookup is optional and starts only when you press “Get historical rate”. It sends the two currency codes and receipt date to Frankfurter, which also sees your IP address. Your pictures, merchants, amounts and receipt numbers stay on this device.

The default is the most common document currency; ties use the first one encountered. A currency you choose manually stays selected.

Choose one final currency for the email grand total. Every document uses this choice; changing it here or on a document updates the whole batch and clears rates and confirmations.

Your pictures and editable details will appear here.
