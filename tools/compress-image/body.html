    2 Say how big it may be

    This is the part other compressors get backwards. They give you a quality slider and leave you to guess which setting lands under the limit you were given. Name the limit instead, and the tool searches for the highest quality that fits it — then spends whatever is left of the budget rather than handing back a file half the size you asked for.
