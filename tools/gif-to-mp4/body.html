2 See what will be written

Every frame of the GIF becomes one frame of video, shown for exactly as long as the GIF shows it. Nothing is resampled, dropped or doubled, and the video plays for as long as the GIF does. The picture stays its own size.

A video does not loop by itself the way a GIF does: the player decides. The preview below loops; a chat app or a feed usually does too.
